- **SYNC-RECOVER-01 (found September 19; implemented October 2026):** `SyncPlayer` restarts from the entry after a fatal player error with 1/2/4-second waits, but `recovery.attempts` was never reset after a successful reconnect, so the fourth fatal error over a long session stopped playback with "The stream stopped" even when each earlier reconnect worked. The retry allowance now renews after 30 seconds of continuously qualified media progression, instead of the 60 seconds of `playing` suggested earlier. Qualified means unpaused, not seeking/ended, readyState ≥ 3, and media advancing consistently with a monotonic clock at the playback rate. Playing events and timers alone never renew it. Frozen/jumping media, clock gaps, waiting/stalls, pause, seeking, ended, errors and restarts reset it. The 20-second startup/stall watchdog and three rapid retries are unchanged, and Stop stays available during retry backoff. Real-device long-session behavior remains under CONTINUITY-01.
- **SYNC-RECOVER-02 (found September 19; implemented October 2026):** a reconnect restarted at the live edge, discarding the delay the listener set. Sync now freezes its last healthy alignment sample (playing UTC, monotonic time, incoming-edge UTC and fragment identity) across failed restarts and restores sample UTC plus elapsed monotonic time on the reloaded playlist. That keeps the real-time delay instead of the old absolute timestamp. Restoration requires a shared, identity-matched fragment with unbroken old and new continuity paths, a consistent incoming-edge delay and a sample under 120 seconds old. The sample, target and clipped incoming edge must each map uniquely in both directions (UTC to media position and back) inside the clipped seekable window, so tolerated rounding never seeks into an overlap or gap. One 10-second deadline per restart allows at most one corrective reseek and produces one outcome: restored (a completed seek or qualified progress observed at the target), fallback or canceled. Fallback copy depends on the case:
  - Before any seek is issued: says audio plays from the incoming broadcast.
  - After an issued seek: makes no position claim.
  - No verified sample: neutral guidance.
  - Missing timestamps or identity: says so.
  - Invalid chronology or edge: generic copy.

  Expired or gapped targets are never clamped or interpolated. On connected audio, user seeks, Jump to incoming, pause and native-control pointer/keyboard gestures cancel restoration. During unloaded retry backoff, in-page movement is disabled; native-control pointer/keyboard gestures still cancel and Stop remains available. Later intent clears a completed recovery notice. Listeners may briefly hear incoming audio first, and post-seek loading adds delay. TV alignment is not guaranteed. Server-side, game capabilities now last six hours and expired ones answer 403, so expiry no longer causes this during a game; provider hiccups still do. Real-browser/phone acceptance remains open under DEVICE-01, CONTINUITY-01 and TIMING-01.
