How automatic sync works

The app compares several TV clock readings with a consistent sequence of running-clock calls in Duke’s commentary, then adjusts the radio automatically. Keep the camera on the scoreboard while it finds a match.

This is an experimental timing estimate. Sparse commentary, stopped clocks, and announcer delay can prevent a match or affect accuracy. Homestream-equivalent accuracy has not been verified.

TV provider is not required. Audio and camera images are processed locally; recognition models download on first use.
